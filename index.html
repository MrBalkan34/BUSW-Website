How a project works

The same three steps for every service.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send an inquiry or an email. You do not have to design anything first. If you prefer, plan your website in the builder and send the project brief.

  2. We confirm scope and price

    BUSW reviews your request and confirms the scope and price with you before any work starts. Estimates from the builders are estimates, and taxes are itemized in the final quote.

  3. We build and deliver

    BUSW builds the project. The base website package includes two consolidated revision rounds and set-up on one hosting account.
