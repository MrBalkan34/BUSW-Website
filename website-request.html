How the builder's estimate works

The builder follows the same price list for everyone. These are its rules, not a quote.

The base website

  • One template-based home page with up to six sections
  • Your text and images, with layouts for desktop and mobile
  • One standard contact form, plus basic page titles and descriptions
  • Deployment to one hosting account
  • Two consolidated revision rounds
  • Mobile support, HTTPS, basic accessibility and correction of defects in delivered work

Optional extras

Additional pages and sections, custom page layouts, copywriting, image preparation, photo galleries, an advanced inquiry form, booking integration, blog or CMS setup, extra languages, analytics, business email setup, content migration and extra revision rounds. Monthly hosting or website care is optional.

Some work is never priced automatically. Online stores, customer accounts, payments, custom databases, API integrations, complex animations and a custom redesign of the home page are quoted separately.

The final quote

The builder shows an estimate, not a final price. BUSW confirms the scope and price after reviewing your request, before any work starts. Taxes are not included and are itemized in the final quote.

Prices follow the business country you choose: US dollars for the United States, Turkish lira for Türkiye and euros everywhere else.

Common questions

Do I have to use the builder?

No. You can send an inquiry or an email without designing anything. The builder is an option for people who want to plan their website first.

What does the builder send to BUSW?

Nothing, for now. Online submission is not connected. The builder saves your work on your own device, and you download a project brief and email it to contact@busoftwareworks.com as an attachment. BUSW only learns about your project when that email arrives.

Does the builder publish my website or take payments?

No. BUSW builds, checks and deploys the finished website after the scope and price are confirmed with you.

How many revisions are included?

The base website includes two consolidated revision rounds. Additional rounds are an optional extra: one consolidated list of changes within the agreed scope.

What is not included in the price?

Taxes, domain registration, email subscriptions, paid plugins, stock images and other assets, translation services and other third-party fees. Work outside the agreed scope is charged by the hour, and BUSW agrees the hours with you before doing it.

What about hosting and maintenance?

The base website is deployed to one hosting account. Two optional monthly plans exist: managed static-site hosting, or website care, which includes hosting plus up to 30 minutes of content edits a month. Unused time does not roll over.

Can BUSW build an online store, customer accounts or payments?

These are quoted separately, together with custom databases, API integrations and complex animations. Describe what you need in an inquiry.

Who approves the price and scope?

You and BUSW, together. BUSW confirms the scope and price with you after reviewing your request, before any work starts.
